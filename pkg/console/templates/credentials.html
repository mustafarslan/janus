enrol this device

Your browser will ask you to confirm with the authenticator. Nothing leaves it but a public key — the private half never exists outside the device, which is the property that makes a stolen session insufficient.

This needs a secure context: navigator.credentials exists only over https, or on localhost. Over plain http the button will say so rather than failing quietly.
